The societal and human implications of digital transformation (DX)

Overview

Digital transformation is changing how we live and work. While it makes daily tasks faster and more convenient, it also creates a gap for people who are not used to modern technology.

Digitalization

Good impacts

  • Faster communication and easy access to information from anywhere.
  • Automation of daily routines and paperwork saves time.

Bad impacts

  • Digital divide: Through volunteering at Computeas in Durham Clayport Library, I noticed many elderly people struggle as basic services move online.
  • Less face-to-face interaction and higher risks of online scams and privacy issues.
